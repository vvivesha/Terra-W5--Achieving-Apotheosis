- play the game!
- follow GitHub Release's instructions for if you are stuck on a blue "Windows Projected Your PC" screen.

## How to play: 
- Left and Right arrow keys to move
- need a little boost? Press the spacebar for x3 movement (once, and only left/right. Use left/right arrow keys to use the boost!)
   - Beware! There's a 3-second cooldown on the spacebar's bosoter.
- dodge the red blocks of lava as they fall! See how many levels you can reach :)
- screenshot your highest level to save it, once the level-up screen pops up.

## Additional game rules:
- you cannot exit the left and right boundaries of your screen.
- if you try to leave such zone, you will be booted in the opposite direction muahahahahahha >:)
